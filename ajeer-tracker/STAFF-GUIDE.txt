AJEER EXPIRY TRACKER FOR MAC

Unzip the matching Mac build and open Ajeer Tracker.app.
It automatically creates Ajeer Documents on your Desktop.
Put your Ajeer PDFs there, or use Add PDFs. Allow Desktop access if macOS asks.
Keep the app open: it checks every 30 seconds and checks again when reopened.

Within 30 days INCLUDES Within 15 days. Do not add the two totals together.
Expired documents and review items are excluded from these upcoming totals.
Scanned PDFs need confirmation against the original before they count.
Duplicate permit + Iqama + expiry copies count once. Different permits count separately.
Supporting pages and unclear files stay visible under Needs review.
Move superseded permits outside the monitored folder for a current-only list.

Double-click a row to open its PDF. Review / correct selected confirms its details.
Export displayed list saves a CSV for Excel or Numbers.
Back up the whole Ajeer Documents folder, including .ajeer-tracker.
Each Mac keeps separate data unless you transfer that folder.

No Python, Homebrew, Word or Excel installation is required on the staff Mac.
The app works offline and does not upload documents or verify them with Ajeer.
Dates use the Mac clock. This app does not monitor while closed.

Builds target macOS 15 or newer. Use arm64 for Apple silicon, x86_64 for Intel.
The build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple Developer ID notarized. Your organisation's
Mac security policy may restrict it. Ask IT if it is blocked; do not disable protection.
